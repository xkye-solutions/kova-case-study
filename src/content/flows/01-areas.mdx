The strip at the far left switches between **Kova**, **Kova Docs** and, for Owners, **Admin**. Each Area has its own navigation and remembers where you left it, so moving from an issue to a document and back is one press that keeps your place.

<Decisions>

- **No top bar anywhere.** The bell, search and the fold control moved into the panel header, so the content gets the full height.
- **Home answers “what needs me?”** with your open issues, anything waiting on your review, and the running Sprints, each with its progress counted from the issues themselves.
- **Admin shows its count on the strip** whenever something is waiting on an Owner.

</Decisions>
