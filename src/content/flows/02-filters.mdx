Narrow any issue list by Status, Priority, Assignee, Project or Placement. Values within one field are alternatives, and fields narrow together. The whole Filter is carried in the address.

<div class="addr-demo" aria-label="An example Filter address">/issues?<wbr /><b>status</b>=in_progress,<wbr />blocked&amp;<wbr /><b>assignee</b>=me</div>

<Decisions>

- **“Me” and “Unassigned” are values,** not special views, so “my blocked work” is just a Filter.
- **A Saved filter is named and private.** It shows up in your own panel and nobody else’s.
- **The keyboard is a first-class path:** `j` and `k` move through the rows and `Enter` opens one.

</Decisions>
